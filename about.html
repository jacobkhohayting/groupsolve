About

Learning through discussion.

GroupSolve runs free online mathematics classes where students explore unfamiliar ideas, explain their reasoning, and solve problems together.

What we do

Focused lessons. Serious mathematics.

Classes combine short explanations with problems and discussion. Topics range from contest foundations to number theory, combinatorics, geometry, and proof.

Students can also submit written solutions for feedback on correctness, clarity, and rigor.
